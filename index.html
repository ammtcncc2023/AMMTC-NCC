NATIONAL CADET CORPS

Discipline • Leadership • Teamwork

The National Cadet Corps provides students opportunities to develop discipline, confidence, teamwork, leadership and a spirit of service.

Through different activities and programmes, cadets learn responsibility and develop their personality while contributing positively to society.

  • Unity and Teamwork
  • Leadership Development
  • Community Service
  • Confidence and Character
